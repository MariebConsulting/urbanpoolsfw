What the Inspection Delivers

A practical assessment of what is happening now, what can wait, and what should be budgeted next.

Surface Condition

Review of erosion, etching, roughness, staining, visible cracking, hollow or suspect areas, prior coatings, and high-stress zones such as steps, slopes, transitions, and benches.

Remaining-Life Assessment

Expected service life based on visible wear, construction era, bather load, chemistry history, prior repairs, and the condition of the existing finish system.

Management-Ready Action Plan

Photographs, condition notes, recommended timing, repair priorities, and defined options for corrective work, coatings, or complete resurfacing.

Stabilize, Coat, or Replaster

The existing substrate and prior finish determine the correct system—not a one-size-fits-all sales pitch.

Discuss the Right Scope

Corrective Stabilization

After draining, suspect areas are mapped and evaluated. Localized failures can be removed and repaired with compatible materials before full-surface preparation.

Protective Coating Systems

Commercial epoxy, water-based commercial coatings, and ecoFINISH hybrid composite systems are evaluated around use, downtime, comfort, chemistry exposure, and budget.

Full SGM Replastering

When the finish or bond is beyond stabilization, we define the demolition, preparation, bond coat, finish system, startup, and long-term maintenance requirements.

Plan downtime before the surface forces it.

Qualifying stabilization and coating scopes may return a drained pool to service much faster than a full renovation. Final timing is set after the surface and existing coating system are verified.
